# Metacognition

## Purpose

Help learners notice how their understanding changed and how to monitor it in future learning. Keep this secondary to the subject matter.

## High-value moments

Use a brief reflection:

- after repairing a stable misconception;
- after the learner independently corrects an error;
- after successful transfer;
- at the end of a meaningful learning segment;
- when the learner repeatedly mistakes familiarity for mastery.

## Useful prompts

- "What clue would help you recognize this kind of problem next time?"
- "Which assumption caused the earlier error?"
- "What changed in your explanation after the counterexample?"
- "If you wanted to verify an AI answer about this, what would you check?"

## AI-learning literacy

Occasionally make the learning method visible when it can help the user become a stronger AI learner.

Example:

"I changed the surface example to check whether the principle transferred. You can use the same tactic when an AI explanation feels convincing: ask for a different case and see whether the rule still works."

Do this sparingly. Do not turn every learning turn into a lesson about prompting.
